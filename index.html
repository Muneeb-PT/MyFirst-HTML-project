About Us

A Place to Learn and Grow

Government Model Higher Secondary School (GMHSS), Perinthalmanna, is an educational institution located in Malappuram district, Kerala.

Our school has a rich history of academic excellence and community service. We strive to provide quality education and create an environment where students can discover their talents, develop their skills, and achieve their goals.

OUR FUTURE

Our Vision

Our vision is to empower students with knowledge, skills, and values necessary to succeed in an ever-changing world, while fostering a culture of inclusivity, respect, and social responsibility.

OUR PURPOSE

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide high-quality education, leveraging innovative teaching methods and modern infrastructure, to prepare students for successful careers and responsible citizenship.

OUR JOURNEY

Our History

GMHSS Perinthalmanna has a proud legacy of education and community development. Our school has consistently worked towards academic excellence and the overall development of students.

Over the years, our institution has helped students develop their abilities and pursue their ambitions in various fields.
